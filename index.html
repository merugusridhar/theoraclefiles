The complaint

Bill the client by the hour, pay the employee for the hour.

His accountHe says he worked more than 16 hours a day for three to four months without overtime pay, and argues that if clients are billed hourly, employees should be paid for the extra hours.

His accountHe says he was terminated within a week of raising the issue. He seeks pending overtime dues, interest and penalties.

The Logical Indian reports that he went to the NHRC and then the Telangana Labour Department. Labour officials acknowledged the complaint and said it is under review, without further comment. Siasat gives his age as 45 and the long days as lasting three to four months.

Ten demands

  1. Six months' net salary as severance pay
  2. No arbitrary termination; due process must be followed
  3. Right to disconnect: no work after working hours
  4. 8-hour workday and overtime pay
  5. Fair performance improvement plan (PIP)
  6. Flexible work-from-home option
  7. Notice period has to be mutual
  8. Compensation for unlawfully terminated employees
  9. Manager's performance also evaluated by team members
  10. Labour commissioner cases resolved within 45 days
Protests and escalation, 2026

What happened, in dates

  1. 19 Feb

    He shares a consolidated list of coverage of his complaint on LinkedIn, naming The Times of India, Mint, The Hans India and Hyderabad Mail.

  2. Late Mar

    He tells PTI he was let go in September after protesting a 16-hour shift, and that Indian staff in the US were hit harder by Oracle's cuts. The story runs on ETV Bharat, The Wire, Outlook Business, Upstox, Storyboard18, News24 and Shafaqna. The figure of about 12,000 India layoffs is attributed to affected employees, and ETV Bharat says Oracle declined to comment.

  3. 1–2 Aug

    Peaceful protest at Dharna Chowk, Hyderabad, under the Corporate Mazdoor Union banner with ten demands. Covered by hmtv, Tholi Velugu and Signal TV Telugu.

  4. 17 Aug

    Road sit-in with police present. The same day M9 News reports his allegation that he received death threats and hid in Delhi for nearly two months, and that he may launch a Corporate Janata Party and protest at Jantar Mantar.

    M9 News · its headline is phrased as a question and the allegations are noted as unverified
  5. 21 Aug

    Outreach to commuters at a metro station near Raheja Mindspace.

  6. 18 Sep

    Sakshi Family premieres two episodes about him and Oracle.

  7. 21–22 Sep

    Sit-in on the driveway outside an Oracle campus, with placards carrying the Oracle Files name. A man in a white shirt with a black armband speaks to camera.

  8. 25 Sep

    Sit-in at Gate 3 and a march with red union flags. A separate clip shows a speaker beside an AITEU banner.

Campaign framing

Names the movement uses

Then, a freedom struggle. Now, a fight for dignity.

Freedom Struggle 2.0 casts the campaign as a modern successor to the independence movement, with employees demanding fair dues and dignity at work.

Janayak is the campaign's name for him, shown on its portrait artwork.

Mancherial to Memphis is a poster that cites the Singareni coal miners as inspiration, with the slogan "Workers are not machines".

The Gen Z Movement is poster art that sets the Civil Rights Movement beside the Oracle Files, under the line "The fight for justice continues".

The posters are stylised or AI-generated artwork, not documentary photographs.
